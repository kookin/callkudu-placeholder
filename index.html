Why Call Kudu

Features

Not another voicemail. Coverage that answers, books, captures interest, and hands over when a human is needed.

Phone line (Voice)

Link to Kudu Canonical Engine, which is the AI part that is trained on your specific business information. Choose your agent's accent and character to suit your business. Handle enquiries, bookings and capture every lead.

WhatsApp (Chat)

The same magic can also handle WhatsApp chats. Here you can use WhatsApp for automated enquiry replies from your website, or customers can directly interact with your WhatsApp line for automated support, sales, bookings and lead capture.

Transparency and Control

Your lines can be toggled on and off for ease of control. All customer interactions are recorded, logged and special requests such as callbacks, messages and escalations are conveniently tracked for your follow up.

Simple Management

Setting up and training is very simple, intuitive and requires no technical knowledge. Agent instructions can be changed at the click of a button. Integrations to your existing calendars and CRM systems can be set up.
