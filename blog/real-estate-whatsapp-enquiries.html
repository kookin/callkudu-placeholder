The Problem with Property24’s ‘WhatsApp Agent’

24 August 2026 · 5 min read

For a lot of South African home buyers, “Chat with agent” on well-known property sites (like Property24) is the only conversation.

Currently the setup is like this: after a chat enquiry is submitted, it starts with a delayed, half-hearted WhatsApp chat — a survey. A placeholder. Until the agent can actually answer. If this is on a weekend, an off-day, or during a busy day of viewings — tough luck. Potential home buyers have to wait.

That gap is where opportunity quietly disappears.

A comprehensive sales study reveals that answering a sales enquiry within the first five minutes makes a lead 21 times more likely to qualify and up to 100 times more likely to connect compared to waiting 30 minutes.

WhatsApp is the real front desk

Estate agencies still put a phone number on the board, but day-to-day contact has moved into WhatsApp. Property24 leans into that with a chat button that drops the buyer straight into a message thread with the agency. Same story on Private Property and a lot of Facebook Marketplace leads — the buyer expects a reply in the app they already live in.

Fair enough. WhatsApp is quick, it’s written down, and you can send a photo of the kitchen without scheduling a call. The problem isn’t the channel. It’s that the person who’s meant to reply is usually mid-showing, mid-valuation, or already three chats deep with other buyers.

So the blue ticks sit there. Or the message gets buried under a group chat. By the time you tap back, they’ve booked with someone else who answered at lunch.

Perspective of the Real Estate Agent

You’re stuck with a full day of viewings. A buyer opens your listing on Property24, hits chat with agent, and WhatsApp fires off: “Is this still available? Can we view Saturday morning?”

In most offices that message either waits for you, or gets bounced to a PA who then has to find you. Meanwhile the buyer has already messaged two other agents on the same street.

Night and weekend browsing makes it worse. People scroll Property24 after supper. Your phone buzzes at 21:40. If the reply waits until morning, you’ve already lost the urgency that made them enquire in the first place.

Why this tends to mean more houses sold

You’re not inventing demand. You’re catching the demand that already tapped your listing and got tired of waiting. Agencies that reply on WhatsApp while the buyer is still looking usually book more Saturdays. More Saturdays mean more second viewings. More second viewings mean more offers on the table when the seller asks what you’ve done this month.

It also changes how you use your day. Less ping-pong on “is it still available?” Less guilt about the unread chats after a double show day. More time for the parts of the job that actually need you — mandate meetings, negotiations, reading the room at a viewing.

Sellers notice it too. An agency that never goes quiet on portal enquiries looks like an agency that will look after their sale.
